## Why

agy updated to 1.3.4 on 2026-10-13. Verified by the session on 2026-10-14:

- **Gemini 4 Argon** ships in agy as `Gemini 4 Argon (Low|Medium|High)`, slugs `gemini-4-argon-low|-medium|-high`. Antigravity offers it on Google AI Ultra and Enterprise only ([antigravity.google/docs/models](https://antigravity.google/docs/models/)). On the operator's Pro plan, `agy --model "Gemini 4 Argon (Low)"` exits 1 with "not recognized as a known model", in 0 s and with no tokens spent.
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro** is Enterprise-only now (same page). `agy models` no longer lists it, and `--model "Gemini 3.1 Pro (Low)"` fails in the same way. The catalog's two 3.1 Pro ids are dead on Pro, and so is the routing tie-breaker, which names 3.1 Pro (High).
- **GPT-OSS-120b** "will be removed on November 2, 2026" (same page).
- **Argon's figures.** All read 2026-10-14.
  - DeepMind model card, published 2026-10-13 ([deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-argon](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-argon/)):

    | Benchmark | Argon | GPT-6 Astra | Opus 5.5 |
    |---|---:|---:|---:|
    | DeepSWE v1.1 | 77.9 | 74.1 | 74.2 |
    | FrontierSWE v2 | 55.0 | 65.5 | 62.3 |
    | Terminal-bench 4.0 | 57.4 | — | 66.4 |

    The same card gives 1M tokens for input and output together, a knowledge cutoff of March 2026, text, image, audio and video in, and text out.
  - Artificial Analysis ([comparison with GPT-6.1 Sol](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/comparisons/gemini-4-argon-vs-gpt-6-1-sol)): Argon (High) Index 53 at $1.99 per Index task. GPT-6.1 Sol (Max) scores 52 at $0.72. The per-benchmark rows (AA, all at High for Argon; Flash High, Sol Max, Opus 5.5 Max) are in the ledger, 2026-10-14.
  - Google's guidance for the thinking levels: Medium is the recommended default; Low for simple or cost-sensitive tasks; High for hard reasoning, maths, science and demanding code ([ai.google.dev](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/latest-model#understanding-thinking-levels)). This is from the research report and was not reopened by the session; candidate.
- **Not confirmed, and so not written anywhere:**
  - the "introductory price through November 30" (the AA page names none);
  - how agy maps the three Argon efforts to thinking budgets;
  - whether Argon draws on the Gemini pool or a separate bucket on Ultra;
  - the date Pro gets Argon (the operator expects it soon; no source found).

## Catalog (`units/gemini/models.js`)

**Entries.** Three Argon entries replace the two 3.1 Pro entries in the same place: after 3.8 Flash and before the Claude entries. They are `family: 'gemini'` and `tier: 'heavy'`, with efforts Low, Medium and High.

What each entry's `useFor` and `avoid` say:
- **High**:
  - `useFor`:
    - multi-step automation and agent workflows: AutomationBench-AA 78, the best of the four compared (Opus 5.5 Max 70, Sol Max 65, Flash High 60);
    - hard knowledge and science research where Flash falls short: HLE 57 vs 48, SciCode 62 vs 57, AA-Omniscience 42 vs 30 (fewer confident errors);
    - terminal agents, where Flash collapses: Terminal-Bench 4.0 57 vs 20;
    - repository-level code tasks: DeepSWE v1.1 77.9, Google-published;
    - the tie-breaker.

    Flash High spends more tokens per task (71k output against Argon's 62k), so Argon's depth does not cost more tokens.
  - `avoid`:
    - PDF and document-heavy knowledge work: GDP.pdf 22, against Sol 31 and Opus 26 — route to Codex;
    - long-context retrieval: AA-LCR 80, level with Flash's 81 — no reason to go up;
    - the hardest software engineering: FrontierSWE v2 55.0 vs Astra 65.5 — route to Codex;
    - frontier physics: CritPt 27 vs Sol 32;
    - quick lookups: Flash Low.

    Opus 5.5 (Max) leads Argon on almost everything but AutomationBench, at three times the cost per task.
- **Medium** and **Low**: no published figures. Artificial Analysis measured only High. Google's guidance (candidate): Medium is the default level for most coding and agent work, Low is for simpler or cost-sensitive tasks. The entries say they inherit High's profile, unmeasured.

**Lost capability.** The 2M window leaves with 3.1 Pro. Argon's window is 1M, shared by input and output. Nothing in the catalog now takes inputs past 1M tokens, and the header says so in one sentence.

**GPT-OSS 120B (Medium).** Its `avoid` gains: "Antigravity removes it on 2026-11-02; the release after that date takes it out of the catalog."

**Header.**
- "SYNCED TO agy" becomes verified 2026-10-14 on agy 1.3.4, with the three points above (Argon added, 3.1 Pro removed, GPT-OSS's removal date).
- A GENERATION SWEEP 2026-10-14 paragraph in the shape of the 2026-09-03 one: what replaced what, and why. The flagship moved from 3.1 Pro to 4 Argon, and Argon unlike Pro is a code and agentic model.
- The paragraphs about 3.1 Pro's niches go.

**`GUIDE`.**
- The two 3.1 Pro clauses become one Argon clause: the three levels, High as the tie-breaker, and the DeepSWE and AA figures.
- Every remaining mention of "3.1 Pro" in the Flash entries' `avoid` (formal reasoning, the 2M window, MRCR) is rewritten against Argon or dropped where no Argon number exists. Nothing is carried over unlabelled.
