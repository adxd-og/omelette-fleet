**Goal:** Two unit-local items of 0.3.5. (§3) Grok's built-in `outputCap` becomes 10 000 000 — the 0.3.4 release review hit 2 000 000 on an 869 s `grok_code_review` whose stream carried every file Grok read, and the answer survived only because the last message *is* the answer. (§4) `gemini_deep_research` files its result under the models it actually asked for: `ctx.usedModel(...)` is called as soon as `stageModels` resolves and **before the first stage spawns**, so a run that is cancelled or capped on its way through is still filed under the stage models it asked for. `gemini_research` and `gemini_image` keep reporting nothing — their CLI picks the model and never says which — so `(vendor default)` stays their `model:`.

Nothing in `core/unit.mjs` moves: `usedModel` has existed since 0.3.4 (first non-empty string wins, ignored for anything that is not a non-empty string, and an explicit or configured model outranks it in `finish()`).
- `docs/CONFIG.md` gains the new number in two tables and the `outputCap` paragraph, and one clause about the deep-research composite in "What the result spool keeps". `CHANGELOG.md` gains this package's two bullets.
